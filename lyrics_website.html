Clustering Analysis: From Mathematical Patterns to Emotional Episodes

This Sankey diagram presents a fascinating bridge between computational analysis and emotional categorization. Using K-means clustering on lyrical data, we've identified patterns that map to emotional episodes (EDR, CB, PEP). The diagram displays how mathematical clusters correspond to recognizable emotional states, helping us understand the underlying structure of lyrical expression.
